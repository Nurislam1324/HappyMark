
# 🌈 Happy Mark

**Calculate your grades with a smile!** 😊

Happy Mark is a simple and easy-to-use grade calculator that helps students calculate their percentages and estimate their final grades.

## ✨ Features

- 📊 **Quarter Grade Calculator** — calculates your quarter grade using:
  - Formative Assessment (FA) — 25%
  - Summative Assessment for the Unit (SAU) — 25%
  - Summative Assessment for the Term (SAT) — 50%
- 📝 **Percentage Calculator** — calculates your score as a percentage.
- 🎯 **Grade Estimation** — shows your estimated grade based on your percentage.
- ➕ **Multiple Scores** — enter scores separated by plus signs.
- 📱 **Mobile Friendly** — works on phones, tablets, and computers.
- 🌐 **No Installation Required** — use it directly in your browser.

## 📈 Grading Scale

| Percentage | Grade | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 85–100% | 5 | Excellent |
| 65–84% | 4 | Good |
| 40–64% | 3 | Satisfactory |
| 0–39% | 2 | Unsatisfactory |

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Open Happy Mark in your browser.
2. Enter your earned points and maximum points.
3. Calculate your result.
4. Check your percentage and estimated grade.
